CHRONICLE I 天地传言 • 溯源

The Legend of the Cloud-Veiled Basin

✦

Long before mortal maps marked these cliffs, ancient tales spoke of a sanctuary suspended between the earth and the heavens.

Here, tiered timber pavilions and curved tiled roofs embrace the sheer mountain face. When the morning mist rises from the river gorges, the stone lanterns flicker with an ethereal amber glow, illuminating secrets left behind by celestials who once walked among mortals.

“The mountains do not remember the footsteps of armies, but they never forget the whisper of a seeker with an unburdened heart.”

CHRONICLE II 行者孤影 • 寻道

The Lone Wanderer

✦

He arrives with no retinue, no herald, and no blade drawn in pride.

Cloaked against the mountain squalls, the traveller carries only a bamboo staff, an inked journal, and an old brass lantern that hums softly in the presence of ancient jade. He does not seek conquest, treasure, or fame. He walks to listen to what the silent peaks have kept guarded through thousand-year seasons.

Each breath in this altitude is thin, fragrant with wet pine and incense. As the stone stairs wind upward into the cloud forest, his footsteps echo the timeless rhythm of pilgrims who came before.
